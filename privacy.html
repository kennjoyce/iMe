The short version

This website does not track you. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and stores nothing about you in your browser. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us through the enquiry form.

Information you give us

The enquiry form on this site asks for your name, company, position, email address, and optionally your phone number and a message.

When you submit the form, the details are passed to Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which forwards them to us by email. Web3Forms acts as a processor on our behalf and does not use your details for its own purposes. See the Web3Forms privacy policy.

We use what you send only to respond to your enquiry, to arrange a demonstration if you have asked for one, and to carry on any business discussion that follows. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us about our services, and, where a contract follows, the performance of that contract.

Information collected automatically

We do not run analytics on this site. However, some things happen automatically when a web page loads, and you should know about them:

  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records standard server information, including your IP address, when a page is served. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. The typefaces used on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the fonts load. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Video. The iMe film is embedded from YouTube using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, which does not set tracking cookies unless you actually play the video. If you play it, or follow a link out to YouTube, Google’s own terms apply. See the Google Privacy Policy.

None of this is used by us to identify or profile you, and we do not have access to that data.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no browser storage. YouTube may set cookies if you choose to play the embedded film.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for any ongoing business relationship that follows from it. Where no relationship follows, we delete the correspondence within two years. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.
